# Desktop pet packs

## Halloween pack · in development

- **Ghosty** floats around the desktop and watches the cursor.
- **Bat** flaps its wings and hangs upside down to sleep.
- **Tiny Skeleton** rattles as it walks, waves a bony arm, and folds into a pile of bones to sleep. Open **Skeleton.app** to launch it.

Each pet has its own Mac app and remembers its own spot.

## General pack · planned for later

Bunnies are the first pet idea. A bunny could hop between resting spots, twitch its ears when it notices the cursor, and tuck its paws under itself for a nap.
